# Pricing research, July 2026

The public price list is positioned between a strong independent specialist in
Nizhny Novgorod and a small Moscow production team. Aggregator minimums are not
used as the target price because they mix beginner, consumer and commercial
offers.

## Market signals

- Moscow Reels editing: Profi lists offers from roughly 2,000 RUB/hour and
  videoproduction from roughly 7,480 RUB/project.
  https://profi.ru/krasota/montazh-reels/
- Moscow event filming: DIKO Studio publishes a starting point of 7,000
  RUB/hour.
  https://videoproduction-moskva.ru/services/videosemka-meropriyatiy/
- Moscow advertising production: ArtNonstop indicates 35,000 RUB for work with
  supplied materials, 80,000 RUB for a full shoot and 300,000 RUB for an
  expanded production.
  https://www.artnonstop.ru/video-production/videoreklama
- Moscow advertising production: RA Quality publishes production from 50,000
  RUB.
  https://www.ra-quality.ru/design_studio/video_production/
- Nizhny Novgorod event filming: local public offers commonly start around
  5,000 RUB/hour or 20,000 RUB for a four-hour booking, while aggregator
  minimums are lower and do not consistently include commercial preparation.
  https://windlive.ru/services
  https://profi.ru/geo-nnov/krasota/videooperatory-na-meropriyatie/price/
- Nizhny Novgorod business photography: public local offers start around
  10,000 RUB/project; lower hourly offers usually describe basic reporting
  without production preparation.
  https://arbuz-studio.ru/tseny
  https://sutygin.ru/stoimost
- Regular commercial content packages in Russia show a broad range. Small
  one-off shoots begin below 30,000 RUB, while systematic monthly production
  packages can start around 120,000 RUB.
  https://profvideo.ru/wp-content/expanded-mediaplan-profvideo-2026.pdf
