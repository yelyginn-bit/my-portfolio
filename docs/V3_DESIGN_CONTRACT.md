# V3 design contract

## Direction

Premium cyber-brutalism: editorial, technical, cinematic and industrial. No gaming neon, fake camera render or permanent glitch.

## Tokens

- Technical white `#f4f4ef`; alt surface `#e9e9e2`; near black `#101010`; charcoal `#20201e`.
- Brand red `#ef3f32`; hot orange `#ff5a1f`; micro violet `#7c5cff`.
- Display: condensed system grotesk; body: contemporary grotesk; utility: system monospace.
- Grid: 12 columns, max 1720px, fluid 10–20px gap.
- Borders: one-pixel raw rules; radius is reserved for physical shells/media modules.

## Components

- Navbar: rounded outer shell, segmented cells, dark utility and CTA cells, dashed About cell; compact three-cell mobile shell.
- Hero: real showreel poster/player as the product object; technical rail and timecode metadata; no stock or AI camera.
- Portfolio: asymmetric 12-column rows; real 16:9 and 9:16 ratios; Kinescope posters mount near viewport.
- Motion: one marquee, short reveal/scale transitions, no continuous glitch; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential motion.
- Form: grouped four-step brief with separate policy acknowledgement and personal-data consent.
- Footer: cinematic dark panel, orange CTA and outline YELYGINN wordmark.
- Breakpoints: desktop >1100, tablet 768–1100, mobile ≤767; touch removes hover-dependent behavior.

Signature: the segmented hardware shell frames the real showreel like a professional production instrument.
